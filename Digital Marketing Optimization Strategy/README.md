# Digital Marketing Strategy Optimisation
## Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Google Merchandise Store** dataset to evaluate customer behavior, marketing channel performance, customer acquisition, retention, and monetization. The objective is to transform raw e-commerce data into actionable insights that support data-driven marketing decisions.

---

## Situation

The Google Marketing Team in the US faced challenges in understanding the effectiveness of its online business and marketing initiatives. Despite operating a successful e-commerce platform, it was difficult to accurately measure user behavior, promotional performance, customer retention, and the impact of different traffic channels.

The team wanted to answer the following business questions:

- How do users interact with the e-commerce platform?
- How effective are promotional campaigns and discount strategies?
- How does website and mobile experience impact engagement and sales?
- Which geographical markets and customer segments are most profitable?
- How effective are current customer acquisition and retention strategies?
- Which traffic channels generate the highest conversion rates?

---

## Task

As the marketing analyst, the goal was to conduct a detailed SQL analysis of the e-commerce platform and develop actionable strategies to improve customer acquisition, retention, monetization, marketing channel performance, and ultimately increase overall revenue.

---

## Action

- Created a database and merged multiple datasets into a unified analytical table using SQL.
- Performed comprehensive data preprocessing and cleansing to ensure data accuracy and reliability.
- Applied advanced SQL techniques including **string functions, date functions, window functions, aggregations, and CTEs** to analyze customer behavior and website performance.
- Evaluated traffic sources, user engagement, conversion rates, bounce rates, and regional performance to generate business insights.

---

## Results & Recommendations

### Seasonal & Revenue Growth

- Replicate December-style holiday campaigns throughout the year to potentially achieve a **25% uplift in revenue**.

### Customer Acquisition

- Increase Monday website traffic by **10%** through targeted promotions to capitalize on the highest conversion rate.
- Leverage high-traffic Tuesdays and Wednesdays with tailored promotional campaigns.

### Customer Retention

- Improve first-time visitor engagement through personalized content, retargeting campaigns, and loyalty programs to increase repeat visits by **10%**.
- Introduce weekend retargeting and loyalty initiatives to improve off-peak sales by **15%**.

### Mobile Experience

- Optimize the mobile shopping experience through improved user interface, mobile-specific offers, and enhanced payment options, addressing the gap between **25% of sessions** and **5% of revenue**.

### Marketing Channel Optimization

- Expand Referral partnerships and strengthen SEO for Organic Search due to their high conversion and low bounce rates.
- Redesign Social Media, Paid Search, Display, and Affiliate strategies to reduce bounce rates and improve visitor retention.

### Regional Strategy

- Develop localized campaigns targeting high-value regions such as **Washington** and **Illinois** to maximize revenue opportunities.
